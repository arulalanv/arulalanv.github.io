01 / ABOUT

A researcher’s curiosity.
An educator’s commitment.

My work explores how intelligent systems can make sense of visual information—and how students can turn ideas into working solutions.

I teach and mentor in the Department of Computing Technologies at SRM Institute of Science and Technology. My interests span computer vision, video processing and analytics, cloud computing and emerging applications of AI.
